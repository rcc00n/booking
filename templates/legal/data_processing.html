How we process your personal data

This summary explains the information we collect when you create a Malva account and how we keep it safe.

Personal data we collect

  • Identity details such as your full name and preferred contact information.
  • Contact details including email address and phone number for confirmations.
  • Booking metadata like appointment history, receipts, and requested services.

Why we store this information

We use your data to manage appointments, issue confirmations, process payments, and provide follow-up support. Without this information we cannot provide the service you request.

We rely on your consent and the performance of our service contract as legal bases for processing.

How we protect your data

  • Data is encrypted in transit and stored using trusted, access-controlled providers.
  • Only authorised team members who need the information to assist you can access it.
  • We retain booking records for regulatory and financial purposes, then securely archive or delete them.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, or withdraw consent by contacting privacy@malva.example.

If you choose to withdraw consent, we will deactivate your account and remove non-essential information, keeping only the records we must retain by law.
