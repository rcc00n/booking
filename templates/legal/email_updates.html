How we use marketing email updates

This page explains what you can expect when you opt in to receive updates and offers from Malva.

What we send

  • Booking reminders, including changes or follow-up recommendations.
  • Occasional product or service highlights tailored to your preferences.
  • Exclusive promotions for loyal guests—never more than twice per month.

How we protect your inbox

We keep communications concise and relevant. Every message includes a visible unsubscribe link that immediately removes you from marketing mailings while leaving essential booking notifications intact.

Your email is never sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing. We only use trusted providers to deliver messages securely.

Changing your preference

You can opt out at any time via the unsubscribe link or by contacting our support team at hello@malva.example. Opting out does not affect essential booking confirmations.
